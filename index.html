I build language technologies that represent the world's languages, by unpacking the meaning of text and performing evaluations of model behavior.



I am an Assistant Professor at the University of South Florida, where I lead the COMPASS research lab, focused on multilingual language modeling (language technologies for the world's languages), computational semantics (analyzing and representing what text means), and model assessment (evaluating plus explaining model performance).


I am also currently a Faculty Associate at Harvard University's Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society. Previously, I was an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Amherst College, and conducted research at Google, USC, and the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
